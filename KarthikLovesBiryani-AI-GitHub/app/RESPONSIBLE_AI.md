# Responsible use and limitations

- All business, customer, review, order, cost, and intervention records are fictional.
- The simulated after period is not an observed business outcome and does not show causation.
- Retrieved correlations and complaint labels are evidence for investigation, not proof of operational root cause.
- The agent has no external LLM, semantic embedding model, authentication, or real integration.
- Approval and audit are a browser demonstration. Local storage can be edited and is not suitable for production authorization or compliance records.
- Validate food safety, commercial costs, suppliers, and legal requirements with qualified people before applying any real change.
