# KPI definitions and assumptions

- **Average rating:** sum of star ratings divided by review count for the selected synthetic cohort.
- **Low-rating share:** count of 1–3 star reviews divided by review count.
- **Repeat-order share:** generated repeat-flagged orders divided by generated orders. It is not cohort-retention analysis.
- **Illustrative rating difference:** difference between independent synthetic cohorts; not causal and not an actual business result.
- **Costs:** fictional menu and packaging assumptions. Ingredient estimates omit labor, taxes, platform commissions, and overhead; intervention cost estimates are proposals, not realized spending.
